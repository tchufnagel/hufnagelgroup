title: PCL kickoff
date: 2026-09-24
---

The NSF Programmable Cloud Laboratory Test Bed Network officially got underway with a kickoff meeting held at the [Johns Hopkins Bloomberg Center](https://washingtondc.jhu.edu). AIMD-L is the home of AIMD-Net, one of 20 nodes in the new network. Find out more about the kickoff, including a list of all of the nodes,  [here](https://ebrc.org/national-network-of-ai-programmable-cloud-laboratories-summit-pcl-kickoff/).
